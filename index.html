About the Director

Director's Message

Welcome to the Cybersecurity Laboratory — a center of excellence in research and innovation.

DR. Mohammad Nowsin Amin Sheikh

Lab Director & Associate Professor

Dept. of CSE, JUST

Gold Medalist PM Award 2012
Get in Touch

"Excellence in research, innovation in cybersecurity, and dedication to shaping the future of digital security."

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to the Cybersecurity Laboratory, a center dedicated to advancing research, innovation, and capacity building in the field of modern cyber defense. In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, ensuring the security, resilience, and reliability of networked and cyber-physical infrastructures has become more critical than ever.

Our primary research domains include Software-Defined Networking (SDN), Attack Detection and Mitigation, Blockchain Technologies, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS). By integrating these interdisciplinary technologies, we aim to develop intelligent, adaptive, and secure frameworks capable of defending against sophisticated cyber threats.

At the Cybersecurity Lab, we focus on developing efficient security solutions for emerging technologies such as smart cities, IoT-enabled infrastructures, and digital transformation initiatives. We strongly believe that combining AI-driven analytics, blockchain-based trust mechanisms, and SDN-enabled network programmability can significantly enhance the detection, prevention, and mitigation of cyber attacks.

Our lab encourages collaboration among researchers, students, industry partners, and policymakers to foster knowledge sharing and real-world problem solving. I invite researchers, students, and industry professionals to collaborate with us in shaping a secure and trustworthy digital future.

Our Focus

Research Areas

Cutting-edge interdisciplinary research spanning the most critical domains of modern cybersecurity.

Software-Defined Networking

Research on SDN architectures enabling programmable, flexible, and secure network infrastructures with dynamic threat mitigation capabilities.

SDN Security Network Programmability OpenFlow

Attack Detection & Mitigation

Advanced techniques for identifying, classifying, and neutralizing cyber threats including DDoS, intrusion, and zero-day attacks in real-time.

DDoS Detection Intrusion Detection Threat Mitigation

Blockchain Technologies

Leveraging distributed ledger technology to establish trust, ensure data integrity, and create secure decentralized systems for digital ecosystems.

Trust Mechanisms Smart Contracts Decentralized Security

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Applying machine learning and deep learning to create intelligent security systems capable of adaptive threat detection and autonomous response.

Machine Learning Deep Learning AI-driven Analytics

Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)

Securing the convergence of digital computation with physical infrastructure, including smart cities, ICS, and IoT-enabled environments.

IoT Security Smart Cities ICS/SCADA

Digital Transformation Security

Research on securing digital transformation initiatives, protecting emerging technologies and ensuring cyber resilience across modern digital enterprises.

Cloud Security Cyber Resilience Digital Defense
